GENETIC INTERACTION CALCULATOR (FOR REPLICATES)

This calculator is the statistical extension of the Pombe Lab Genetic Interaction Calculator. It uses the same multiplicative (product) model and the same biological interpretation criteria, but allows repeated measurements within each independent experiment and multiple independent experiments performed at different times.

Important: individual ImageJ measurements stay grouped within their experiment. The final statistical analysis uses the independent experiment as the statistical unit, not the number of ImageJ measurements.

EXPERIMENT SETUP

Parent names are defined here. Their ImageJ measurements are entered separately for every concentration and independent experiment.

MEASUREMENT DATA

Within each independent experiment, enter all repeated ImageJ area measurements for WT, each parent, and each combined mutant. The experiment-specific mean WT area is used as the normalization reference for that experiment.

FINAL STATISTICAL RESULTS

Each combined mutant is analyzed separately. The final statistics use one interaction value per independent experiment.

STATISTICAL EXPLANATIONS

What does the one-sample t-test mean?

For each independent experiment, the calculator determines how far the observed fitness of a combined mutant differs from the fitness expected from the multiplicative model. These experiment-level interaction values are then compared with 0, where 0 means that the observed result agrees with the multiplicative expectation.

The one-sample t-test asks whether the average interaction across the independent experiments is statistically distinguishable from 0.

Important limitation: with only a small number of independent experiments, such as 3, statistical power is limited and the result can be strongly influenced by variation between experiments. A non-significant result does not prove that there is no interaction; it means that the available experiments do not provide sufficiently strong statistical evidence for a difference from 0.

What does the 95% confidence interval mean?

The 95% confidence interval shows the uncertainty around the estimated average interaction. It gives a range of values compatible with the observed experiment-level data under the statistical model.

If the interval includes 0, the data do not provide strong statistical evidence that the average interaction differs from the multiplicative expectation. With only a small number of independent experiments, confidence intervals can be quite wide.

What does the quality flag mean?

The quality flag describes how consistently the independent experiments produce the same interaction result. It is different from statistical significance.

The calculator keeps the individual experiment values visible. It does not automatically delete an experiment because it differs from the others. A result can therefore have good reproducibility but insufficient statistical power, or statistical evidence together with poor reproducibility.

What do within-experiment and between-experiment variability mean?

Within-experiment variability describes how consistent repeated ImageJ measurements are on the same plate.

Between-experiment variability describes how consistent the independent experiment-level interaction values are across different experimental days.

These are separate quality questions and are shown separately in the calculator.

MODEL

Within each independent experiment, the arithmetic mean of the WT ImageJ measurements is used as the WT reference. Mean areas for all other strains are normalized to that experiment-specific WT mean: fitness (%) = mean strain area / mean WT area × 100.

Under the multiplicative model, expected combined-mutant fitness is the product of the relative fitnesses of its parent strains. The experiment-level interaction is observed combined-mutant fitness − expected fitness, in percentage points.

The final statistical analysis treats the independent experiment as the statistical unit. Individual ImageJ measurements within an experiment are retained for within-experiment variability assessment but are not counted as independent experiments.
