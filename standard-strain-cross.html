STANDARD STRAIN CROSS

1.
Prepare 30 µL mix of sterile dH2O and both strains (h- h+ 1:1; h90 h+/- 1:2).
2.
Plate the suspension on PMG-N and incubate for 3 days at 25 °C.
3.
Check sporulation in the microscope (look for zygotic asci).
4.
Incubate cells with lytic enzymes (final c = 2 mg/mL) in 500 µL → O/N, 32 °C + vigorous shake.
5.
Determine spore concentration by counting spores in Bürker chamber.
6.
Plate 200–500 spores onto YES plate + 1st antibiotic and incubate at 32 °C (aim for single colonies).
7.
Replate single colonies onto YES plate + 2nd antibiotic. Incubate at 32 °C.
8.
cPCR for both wild-type alleles.
9.
Store freshly grown colonies in YES + 25% glycerol at −80 °C.
10. After double mutant construction, let double mutant sporulate on PMG-N. If you observe azygotic asci, there are diploids in your strain mixture.
11. Alternatively, make a plate containing all needed antibiotics.
12. If crossing auxotrophic strains, prepare suitable selection media.
