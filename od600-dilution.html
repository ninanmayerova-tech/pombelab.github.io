OD600 Culture Dilution

Optical density at 600 nm (OD600) can be used as a convenient measure of the amount of cells in a culture. When preparing a culture with a desired final OD600, the volume of an existing culture required to reach that target can be calculated using the measured OD600 of the starting culture and the desired final volume.

How does the calculation work?

The calculation is based on the relationship between the concentration of the starting culture and the concentration desired in the final culture.

OD₁ × V₁ = OD₂ × V₂

Where:

  • OD₁ = measured OD600 of the starting culture
  • V₁ = volume of starting culture required (mL)
  • OD₂ = desired final OD600
  • V₂ = desired final culture volume (mL)

Rearranging the equation gives the volume of starting culture required:

V₁ = (OD₂ × V₂) ÷ OD₁

The remaining volume is then made up with fresh medium:

Vmedium = V₂ − V₁

Multiple measurements

If several OD600 measurements are available, they can all be entered at once. The calculator will determine the required culture volume and fresh medium volume separately for each measurement.

You can enter the measurements either one per line or separated by commas.

For example, both of the following formats are accepted:

One per line:
0.82
1.15
0.67
0.94

Separated by commas:
0.82, 1.15, 0.67, 0.94

When is the dilution possible?

Dilution with fresh medium can only decrease the OD600. Therefore, the measured OD600 of the starting culture must be equal to or higher than the desired final OD600.

If the measured OD600 is lower than the target OD600, the required volume of starting culture would be greater than the final culture volume. In this case, the desired OD600 cannot be achieved by dilution alone.

Calculation

For each measured OD600 value, the calculator determines:

Culture volume = (Target OD600 × Target volume) ÷ Measured OD600

Fresh medium = Target volume − Culture volume

The target volume, culture volume, and fresh medium volume are all expressed in millilitres (mL).
