IMMUNOSTAINING

Immunostaining of yeast cells

Prepare cell pellets.

All centrifugation steps are performed for 3 min at 5,000 rpm.

  1. Resuspend cell pellets in 0.6 mL YES + 1.2 M sorbitol.
  2. Add 60 µL PFA and mix by inverting the microtube.
  3. Incubate at RT for 45 min.
  4. Centrifuge and wash 3× with 0.5 mL PEMS.
    • Leave the samples in the last wash and freeze at −20 °C.
  5. Centrifuge, remove the supernatant, and resuspend in 0.5 mL PEMS + zymolyase (0.5 mg/mL, freshly prepared).
  6. Incubate at 37 °C for 90 min. Gentle shaking is allowed.
    • Check the cells under the microscope. Cells without cell walls will appear lighter.
  7. Centrifuge, remove the supernatant, and resuspend in 0.5 mL PEMS.
  8. Centrifuge, remove the supernatant, and resuspend in 0.2 mL PEMS.
  9. Add 0.2 mL PEMS + 2% Triton and mix by inverting the microtube.
  10. Incubate for 2 min.
  11. Centrifuge and wash 3× with 0.5 mL PEM.
  12. Centrifuge, remove the supernatant, and resuspend the cells in 0.1 mL PEMBAL.
  13. Incubate on a rotating wheel (4 rpm) for 1 h.
  14. Centrifuge, remove the supernatant, and resuspend the cells in 50–100 µL PEMBAL + 1st antibody.
  15. Incubate overnight (O/N) at RT on a rotating wheel (4 rpm).
  16. Centrifuge, remove the supernatant, and wash 3× with 0.1 mL PEMBAL.
  17. Centrifuge, remove the supernatant, and resuspend the cells in 50–100 µL PEMBAL + 2nd antibody.
  18. Incubate for 5–6 h at RT on a rotating wheel (4 rpm).
    • Light-sensitive! Keep the samples covered.
  19. Centrifuge and wash 3× with 0.1 mL PEM.
  20. Centrifuge, remove the supernatant, and resuspend in 0.1 mL 1× PBS.
  21. Store covered at 4 °C.

Solution Composition

Solution Composition
PFA 380 mg PFA + 700 µL PEM; vortex; 1 min at 65 °C; vortex; add 48 µL 5 M NaOH; vortex; incubate at 65 °C; vortex after each minute. After 2–3 min, PFA should be dissolved.
Super toxic!
PEM (0.75 L) 22.68 g PIPES; 0.2853 g EGTA; 375 µL 2 M MgSO₄; +400 mL dH₂O; adjust with NaOH to pH 6.9; add dH₂O up to 750 mL; filter sterilise.
PEMS (0.3 L) 65.12 g sorbitol; adjust to 300 mL with PEM; filter sterilise.
Sorbitol 2 M (0.25 L) 45.5425 g sorbitol; adjust to 250 mL with dH₂O; filter sterilise/autoclave.
PEMS + 2% Triton (10 mL) 200 µL 100% Triton; adjust to 10 mL with PEMS.
PEMBAL (50 mL) 730 mg lysine (100 mM final), dissolve in PEM; add 500 mg BSA (1% final) and dissolve; add 0.5 mL of 10% NaN₃ (0.1% final; NaN₃ is super toxic); adjust to 50 mL with PEM.
YES + 1.2 M sorbitol (50 mL) 30 mL 2 M sorbitol + 20 mL YES.
