URA4 LOSS ASSAY – PLATING CALCULATOR

PRINCIPLE

This calculator determines how to plate cells from a culture for the ura4 loss assay. The goal is to plate 100,000 cells on YES + 5-FOA and 100 cells on YES as a growth control.

Enter the measured cell concentration in each culture in cells/mL. The calculator determines the volume required to contain the target number of cells and recommends a dilution when the direct plating volume would be too small.

Recommended plating volume: 20–120 µL. Dilutions are selected to keep the final volume in this range, while preferentially using 10-fold dilutions (10×, 100×, 1000×, etc.). If a 10-fold dilution cannot give a suitable volume, a 5-fold or 2-fold dilution is considered.

Each strain contains 12 independent colonies. Add additional strains if needed; every strain always contains 12 colonies.

CALCULATION

Direct volume (µL) = target cells ÷ culture concentration × 1000

The recommended dilution is then applied to the culture before plating. The reported plating volume is the volume of the diluted culture required to contain the target number of cells.

Copy the 12 cell concentrations from Excel and paste them here, one per line or separated by tabs/spaces. The values will be placed into Colony 1–12 automatically.
