URA4 LOSS ASSAY

TF2-6 ura4 loss assay

PRINCIPLE

The ura4+ allele is used as a reporter for genomic rearrangements at the Tf2-6 locus. Cells that lose ura4+ are selected on 5-FOA and subsequently characterized by colony PCR to distinguish between eviction and conversion events.

EXPERIMENTAL SETUP

Starting cultures: 12 independent cultures of each WT and mutant strain are picked from EMMG-ura plates to ensure ura4+ starting populations.

Cultures are seeded in rich medium at the same density, using 20 mL per culture. After growth to saturation, cells are plated onto 5-FOA selective plates to select for ura4 loss. Colonies are subsequently characterized by colony PCR.

Figure 1. Ura4 loss assay scheme, adapted from Zaratiegui et al. (2011).

PROTOCOL

1. Pick 12 independent cultures of the WT and mutant strains from EMMG-ura plates to ensure ura4+ starting populations.
2. Seed the cultures in rich medium at the same density (20 mL per culture).
3. Grow at 32 °C to saturation for 24–26 h with shaking at 160 rpm. Alternatively, grow on plates for 1 week to reach stationary phase. Plate 100,000 cells onto 5-FOA selective plates and grow for 3 days.
4. Plate 100 cells onto rich medium to account for possible cell death and plating efficiency.
5. After colonies arise, count the colonies and pick all colonies from each genotype (minimum 30) for characterization of the rearrangements by colony PCR using primers Tf2-6_F, Tf2-6_R, and Tf2-2_R.

YES + 5-FOA MEDIUM

Final volume: 1 L

The medium is prepared as 0.5 L of dissolved 5-FOA mixed with 0.5 L of 2× YES medium. The final concentration is 1× YES + 5-FOA.

5-FOA solution

Dissolve 1 g 5-FOA in 500 mL sterile H2O.

Important: 5-FOA is light-sensitive. Prepare separately; it takes time to dissolve.

H2O Add to 500 mL
6. Autoclave the 2× YES medium. Cool to 55 °C, then mix 0.5 L of 2× YES medium with 0.5 L of dissolved 5-FOA to obtain 1 L of 1× YES + 5-FOA.
Practical preparation note: Prepare 3 × 0.25 L of 2× YES in 0.5-L flasks and mark the volume. If the media boil too much, use one flask to correct the volumes of the remaining two. Each 0.25 L of 2× YES medium, cooled to 55 °C, is then mixed with 0.25 L of dissolved 5-FOA, giving 0.5 L of 1× YES + 5-FOA (approximately 24 plates).
7. Keep the 5-FOA plates in darkness.
