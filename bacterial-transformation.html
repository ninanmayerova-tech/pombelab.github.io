BACTERIAL TRANSFORMATION

Transformation of competent bacterial cells

Sterile technique

  1. Thaw competent bacterial cells from the −80 °C freezer on ice.
  2. In a 1.5 mL Eppendorf tube, mix 50–100 µL of bacterial cells with the vector (ligation mix).
    • 1/10 V → newly constructed plasmids
    • 0.5–1 µL → basic/verified plasmids
    • + control sample (cells without plasmid)
  3. Incubate the ligation mix for 30 min on ice.
  4. Heat shock for 90 s at 42 °C (specific cells require a specific heat-shock condition – check beforehand!).
  5. Allow the mix to cool for 2–10 min on ice.
  6. Add 1 mL of liquid medium and resuspend the mix.
  7. Incubate the cells for 45 min–1 h at 37 °C with moderate shaking.
  8. Centrifuge the suspension for 30 s–1 min at 8000 rpm.
  9. Discard the supernatant, leaving a volume of 50–100 µL.
  10. Resuspend the pellet and plate it on solid medium containing the appropriate antibiotic(s).
    • 10 µL + 90 µL / 10 µL + 40 µL
    • 50 µL if Gibson assembly was used to prepare the vector
  11. Grow the cells overnight at 37 °C.
  12. The next morning, inoculate one bacterial colony into 4 mL of liquid 2×TY (+4 µL of ampicillin).
    • Prepare multiple colonies.
  13. Incubate the cell cultures overnight at 37 °C in a shaker.
  14. The next morning, isolate pDNA.
    • Plasmid DNA isolation → GeneJET Miniprep Kit
  15. Plasmid verification:
    • Restriction analysis (endonucleases)
    • PCR amplification
  • After transformant colonies have grown, verify the pDNA by colony PCR. After successful verification, prepare overnight cultures and isolate pDNA the following morning.
  • Overgrown bacterial colonies form satellite colonies due to antibiotic degradation, which makes picking a single colony more difficult.
  • Ampicillin degrades after 16–20 h – plan your experiments accordingly.

Media Composition

Medium Composition
LB (1 L) 10 g tryptone; 5 g yeast extract; 10 g NaCl. Dissolve in 950 mL of distilled water; adjust the pH to 7.0 (optional); bring the volume to 1 L; and autoclave to sterilize. Solid plates + 2% agar.
2×TY (1 L) 16 g tryptone; 10 g yeast extract; and 5 g NaCl. Dissolve in 950 mL of distilled water; bring the volume to 1 L; and autoclave to sterilize. Solid plates + 2% agar.
