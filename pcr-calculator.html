PCR CALCULATOR

Theory & practical notes

A PCR master mix contains all components that are common to the reactions. Preparing a master mix helps reduce pipetting errors and improves consistency between samples. The manufacturer's DreamTaq protocol recommends preparing enough master mix for the required reactions plus one extra reaction, aliquoting the mix, and then adding template DNA.

Controls: Never forget to include appropriate positive and negative controls whenever possible. Include your control reactions in the number of reactions entered into the calculator so that enough master mix is prepared.

Reaction setups

The calculator uses the following working reaction volumes:

  • DreamTaq + genomic DNA: 50 µL
  • DreamTaq + plasmid DNA: 50 µL
  • DreamTaq + colony: 12.5 µL
  • Q5 + genomic DNA: 50 µL
  • Q5 + plasmid DNA: 50 µL

DreamTaq genomic and plasmid reactions use a 50 µL working recipe based on the Thermo Scientific manufacturer's recommended 50 µL reaction. The laboratory uses a 10 mM-each dNTP mix, so the dNTP volume is adjusted to give a 200 µM final concentration of each dNTP. Primer concentrations are set to 0.5 µM final using 10 µM primer stocks.

Q5 reactions use the NEB 50 µL reaction setup with 5× Q5 Reaction Buffer, 10 mM dNTPs, 10 µM primers and Q5 High-Fidelity DNA Polymerase.

The colony-PCR recipe is a laboratory-specific 12.5 µL working reaction. The master mix is aliquoted first and the colony template is added last.

Colony PCR boiling step:

Prepare the colony suspension and boil it at 95°C for 5 min. For each reaction, aliquot 10 µL of DreamTaq master mix and add 2.5 µL of the boiled colony suspension. Final reaction volume: 12.5 µL.

Extra master-mix volume

To reduce losses during pipetting, the calculator automatically adds extra reaction volume:

  • Fewer than 10 samples: +0.5 reaction
  • 10 or more samples: +1 reaction

Calculation

The calculator determines the required amount of each master-mix component by multiplying the volume required for one reaction by the number of reactions, including the automatically added extra volume.

1. The number of reactions entered by the user is determined.

2. An additional 0.5 reaction is added for fewer than 10 reactions, or 1 additional reaction for 10 or more reactions.

3. The volume of every master-mix component is multiplied by the calculated number of reactions.

4. The master mix is aliquoted into individual reactions and the template is added separately.

Total component volume = volume per reaction × calculated reactions

The template volume is not included in the master-mix total. It is added individually to each reaction after the master mix has been aliquoted.

References

DreamTaq DNA Polymerase. Thermo Scientific. The manufacturer's protocol specifies a 50 µL reaction containing 5 µL 10× DreamTaq Buffer, 5 µL of 2 mM-each dNTP mix, 1.25 U DreamTaq polymerase, primers at 0.1–1.0 µM, template DNA at 10 pg–1 µg, and water to 50 µL. The protocol also recommends preparing master mix for the required reactions plus one extra reaction and adding template afterward.

Thermo Scientific DreamTaq DNA Polymerase protocol

Q5 High-Fidelity DNA Polymerase. New England Biolabs (NEB). The 50 µL reaction contains 10 µL 5× Q5 Reaction Buffer, 1 µL 10 mM dNTPs, 2.5 µL each of 10 µM forward and reverse primers, 0.5 µL Q5 polymerase and variable template DNA, with water to 50 µL.
