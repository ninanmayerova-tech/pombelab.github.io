GENETIC INTERACTION CALCULATOR

This calculator is designed for screening genetic interactions in Schizosaccharomyces pombe using the multiplicative (product) model. ImageJ area measurements are converted to fitness relative to the wild type at the same cell / spot concentration, and the expected fitness of a combined mutant is calculated from the parent strains measured at that same concentration.

Workflow: define the experimental setup once, then enter the ImageJ measurements for each cell / spot concentration. For another plate with the same setup, use the existing structure and replace only the measurement values.

INTERPRETATION CRITERIA

The categories below are the interpretation criteria used for this Pombe Lab calculator. They are lab-specific biological criteria and should not be treated as universal thresholds.

Strong negativedifference < −20 percentage points
Weak negative−20 to −10 percentage points
No significant genetic interaction> −10 to < +10 percentage points
Weak positive+10 to +20 percentage points
Strong positive> +20 percentage points
The screening calculator reports quantitative differences and descriptive variation (SD and range). It does not force a statistical significance test onto the screening data.

LEGEND

WTWild type
SDStandard deviation — the spread of observed fitness values around their mean.
ppPercentage points — the difference between observed and expected fitness.

MODEL

Fitness is calculated relative to the wild type at the same concentration: fitness (%) = strain area / WT area × 100. Under the multiplicative model, the expected fitness of a combined mutant is the product of the relative fitnesses of its parent strains. The interaction difference reported here is mean observed fitness − expected fitness.

A positive difference means that the combined mutant grows better than expected from the multiplicative model, whereas a negative difference means that it grows worse than expected.
