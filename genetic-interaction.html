GENETIC INTERACTION CALCULATOR

This calculator is designed for screening genetic interactions in Schizosaccharomyces pombe using spot-test growth measurements. ImageJ/FiJi area measurements are converted automatically to relative fitness using wild type as the 100% growth reference.

Multiplicative model

The expected fitness of a combined mutant is calculated using the multiplicative (Product) model. The fitness values of the individual parental mutants are multiplied to obtain the expected fitness of the combined mutant.

Expected fitness = W1 × W2 × ... × Wn

The interaction is then quantified as the difference between the observed and expected fitness:

Difference = Observed fitness − Expected fitness

A positive difference means that the combined mutant grows better than expected, whereas a negative difference means that it grows worse than expected.

Screening interpretation

The interaction categories used by this calculator are the Pombe Lab screening criteria. They are intended to identify effects that may be biologically relevant in the screening experiment. In particular, differences of 10–20% should not be overlooked because relatively small genetic effects may still be biologically meaningful.

  • ≤ −20 percentage points: strong negative interaction
  • > −20 to ≤ −10 percentage points: weak negative interaction
  • > −10 to < +10 percentage points: non-interacting
  • ≥ +10 to ≤ +20 percentage points: weak positive interaction
  • > +20 percentage points: strong positive interaction

Statistical analysis

Statistical testing is not performed in this screening calculator. Each combined strain is measured once, while the expected fitness is calculated from single measurements of the parental strains. A more advanced statistical analysis will be included in the separate calculator for repeated experiments and technical/experimental replicates.

Interpretation

The interaction category is based on the difference between the mean observed fitness of the combined strains and the multiplicative expected fitness. The numerical difference is reported in percentage points. The category reflects the Pombe Lab screening criteria and should be interpreted together with the individual strain values and their heterogeneity.
