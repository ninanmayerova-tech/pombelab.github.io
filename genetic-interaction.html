GENETIC INTERACTION CALCULATOR

This calculator is designed for screening genetic interactions in Schizosaccharomyces pombe using the multiplicative (product) model. ImageJ area measurements are converted to fitness relative to the wild type, and the expected fitness of a combined mutant is calculated from the fitness of its parent strains.

Workflow: define the experimental setup once, enter the ImageJ measurements from the current plate, and the calculator updates all fitness values and interpretations automatically. When analysing another plate with the same setup, only the measurement values need to be changed.

INTERPRETATION CRITERIA

The categories below are the interpretation criteria used for this Pombe Lab calculator. They are lab-specific biological criteria and should not be treated as universal thresholds for genetic interaction studies.

Strong negative difference < −20 percentage points
Weak negative −20 to −10 percentage points
No significant genetic interaction > −10 to < +10 percentage points
Weak positive +10 to +20 percentage points
Strong positive > +20 percentage points
The current screening calculator reports quantitative differences and descriptive variation (SD and range). It does not force a statistical significance test onto the screening data.

MODEL

Fitness is calculated relative to the wild type: fitness (%) = strain area / WT area × 100. Under the multiplicative model, the expected fitness of a combined mutant is the product of the relative fitnesses of its parent strains. The interaction difference reported here is: mean observed fitness − expected fitness.

A positive difference means that the combined mutant grows better than expected from the multiplicative model, whereas a negative difference means that it grows worse than expected.
